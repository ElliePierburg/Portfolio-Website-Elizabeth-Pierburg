SAFE Robotics Lab

Role: Mobile Manipulator Platform Team   |   Organization: SAFE Robotics Lab, Georgia Tech

Duration: January 2025 — Present

Project Overview

SAFE Robotics is developing an autonomous mobile robot designed to identify when strawberries are ready to be picked an navigate autonoumously to perform the harvesting task. My work focused on the mechanical and systems integration of the robot, including desgning the mounting interface connecting the robot torso to the mobile base and integrating the platform's mechanical, hardware, and software systems.

System Architecture

  • Platform: Mobile manipulator with robotic arms and mobile base
  • Compute & Sensors: NVIDIA Jetson hardware, LiDAR, and RGB camera
  • Software: ROS and motion-planning software
  • Outputs: Mobile robot movement and produce-picking operation
  • Mechanical Interfaces: Robotic-arm mounts and torso-to-base mounting interface

My Contributions

Platform Mechanical Integration

  • Designed the mechanical interface connecting the robot torso to the mobile base.
  • Conntect the battery, mounting system, and robot into one working system.

Robotics Software & Integration

  • Worked with ROS-based motion-planning software for coordinated robot operation.
  • Supported integration with NVIDIA Jetson Nano hardware, lidar, RGB camera, and robotic-arm systems.

Rocker Pedal Control Interface

  • Designed a rocker-style pedal to provide foot-based robot control without a handheld controller.
  • Designed the pedal in SolidWorks to provide resistance against accidental movement.
  • Manufactured the pedal prototype using 3D printing and iterated its resistance and range of motion.

Skills & Tools

SolidWorks, ROS, NVIDIA Jetson Nano, mechanical design, robotic hardware integration, 3D printing, motion planning.
