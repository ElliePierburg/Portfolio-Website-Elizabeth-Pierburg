Root Cause: Main Switch and Battery

Role: Mechatronics Engineer Co-op   |   Organization: Yamaha Motor Manufacturing Corporation of America

Duration: [Add dates]

Project Overview

Investigated two vehicle reliability problems: recurring main-switch warranty failures and battery discharge during warehouse storage. The work combined environmental testing, electrical measurements, failure analysis, and evaluation of practical countermeasures.

Main Switch Reliability Investigation

  • Investigated recurring warranty failures in vehicle main switches.
  • Designed and assembled test harnesses connecting 16 main switches across four switch types to a power supply.
  • Conducted environmental testing in a metrology/environmental chamber.
  • Evaluated corrosion behavior and tracked internal resistance over time to characterize switch degradation.

Main Switch Contributions

  • Led environmental reliability testing of vehicle main switches.
  • Designed a 16-channel test harness for simultaneous salt-spray testing.
  • Analyzed electrical resistance data to investigate corrosion-related failures.
  • Evaluated potential countermeasures based on reliability improvement and cost.

Battery Reliability & Trickle-Charger Study

  • Investigated battery discharge failures occurring while vehicles were stored in warehouses.
  • Tested different batteries under elevated-temperature storage conditions in an oven/environmental chamber.
  • Recorded battery voltage over time to evaluate performance and discharge behavior.

Battery Study Contributions

  • Researched and selected a trickle-charger solution for vehicles stored in warehouses.
  • Designed the trickle-charger connection from an engineering drawing.
  • Supported purchasing and release of required components and documentation.

Testing & Results

Main Switch Result

The proposed countermeasures would substantially increase cost without providing enough quality improvement. A main-switch redesign was identified as the more appropriate long-term solution.

Battery Result

The battery investigation was completed and supported implementation of a trickle-charger solution for vehicle storage.

Skills & Tools

Reliability testing, electrical resistance measurement, root-cause analysis, test-harness design, multimeters, environmental chambers, power supplies, engineering drawings, data collection.
